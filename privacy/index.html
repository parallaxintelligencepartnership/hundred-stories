How to play Play Requests

Privacy

There is nothing to sign in to and nothing to hand over. This page says exactly what does and does not happen with your data, on the site and in the apps.

B1 · ACCOUNTS AND TRACKING

No accounts, nothing tracked

There are no accounts and no sign-in. The game does not collect, send, or track anything about you or your tower. There are no ads and no analytics in the game.

B2 · YOUR SAVE

Your save is yours

Your tower is saved on your own device: in your browser's storage on the web, and in an app file in the store apps. If you export a save, that export is your own file, and it never reaches us.

B3 · THE ONE NETWORK REQUEST

The one network request

The web pages and the store apps both load two typefaces from Google Fonts when they open. That is a request to Google's servers, subject to Google's privacy policy, and it is the only network request the game makes.

B4 · HOSTING

Hosting

This site is served by a hosting provider, which may log requests the way any web host does.

B5 · WHERE THIS APPLIES

Where this applies

This page covers hundredstories.xyz and the store apps.
