Build a tower. Run it well.

Hundred Stories is a free tower-building simulation with hand-drawn pixel art. Place offices, condos, shops and elevators, keep your tenants happy, and climb from one star to TOWER.

Play in your browser

Free. No account. Nothing uploaded.

What it is

Rules with teeth

Every room, price, elevator limit and stress rule is tuned so a tower can fail. Tenants notice slow elevators, noisy neighbours and dirty rooms, and they leave.

Plays offline

Install it once from your browser and it runs with no connection. Your tower is saved on your device as you play, and you can export it as a file whenever you like.

Yours, and open

There is no account, no server and nothing tracked. The source is public under the AGPL, so you can read exactly how the tower thinks.

How it plays

  1. Start with a lobby and a little cash.
  2. Rent floors to tenants and give them elevators that do not make them wait.
  3. Earn stars as the tower grows. One hundred stories is the ceiling.

Best on a desktop

The game plays best with a mouse and a screen wider than a phone. It loads on a phone, but building is fiddly there for now.
