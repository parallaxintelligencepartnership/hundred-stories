# Look round spec

Date: 2026-09-22. Implements docs/reviews/2026-09-22-modernisation-proposal.md section 4 in five ships, each a delegation to a senior-implementer (Opus 5.5). The Codex Astra notes in docs/reviews/2026-09-22-codex-astra-ui-graphics.md give geometry and palette where this spec cites them. Agents do not commit, bump versions or deploy; the orchestrator does. Every ship: `npm test`, `npm run typecheck`, and the six hashes from `npx vite-node@6.0.0 scripts/bench/hash.ts` unchanged (small 4dee77a3 / 7151c613, medium d3ba899e / e2cafb93, large 823b38bb / d4023cca). Nothing under src/sim changes in this round except where a ship says so.

Screenshots: every ship ends with production-build screenshots so the orchestrator can judge the result. Method: `npm run build`, `npx vite preview --port <free port>`, headless Chrome from a fresh profile with `--headless=new --use-angle=metal --ignore-gpu-blocklist --window-size=1440,900 --hide-scrollbars`, load `/play/?smoke` is DEV only so instead seed the save from the scratchpad tools folder (`save.json`, written into IndexedDB `hundred-stories` / `saves` / `autosave` over CDP, see the `cdp.mjs` there) then load `/play/?cb=<time>`; also a landing shot of `/` at 1440 and at 390 wide (emulate over CDP, `Emulation.setDeviceMetricsOverride`). Save PNGs to the scratchpad folder named in the delegation. Chrome on this Mac: `/Applications/Google Chrome.app/Contents/MacOS/Google Chrome`. If Metal fails to start in headless, fall back to `--use-gl=angle --use-angle=swiftshader --enable-unsafe-swiftshader` and say so (colours are still right, only frame timing is not).

## Ship L1: resolution and scale, structure pass (0.4.0)

1. src/render/art.ts: `TILE_PX` 8 to 16, `FLOOR_PX` 36 to 72. Every drawer that hardcodes pixel offsets is doubled. Do it in two classes:
   a. Authored natively at the new size: `drawShell`, `drawCellOutline`, `drawSlab`, `drawShaft`, `drawCar`, `drawSim`, `drawGhost`, `drawLobby`, `drawSkyLobby`, `drawStairs`, `drawEscalator`, `drawOffice`, `drawHotelSingle`, `drawHotelTwin`, `drawHotelSuite`. Use the Astra section 1 geometry for the shell, office, hotel single, lobby tile and standard car, and extend the same rules to hotel twin and suite, the express and service cars, and sky lobby. Two-tone walls (lit face, and a shadow face on the rightmost 8 interior px, 4 for lobby tiles), the slab at y 66 to 71 with a 2 px top edge and a 4 px 25 percent shadow drawn below it (the slab texture is 4 px taller than today's; the renderer positions it so the shadow overlaps the top of the floor below), 12 by 12 window panes at (2 + 16n, 6) with 2 px mullions and a sill at y 18 to 19, 2 px outlines. Person: a straight double of today's figure, 16 by 48 px (1 by 3 tiles), not the 32 by 64 in the Astra note; keep the two frames, the stress colours and the per-kind accents, drawn with twice the pixels so the figure gets a head, a body and legs rather than a stick. Cars: `SHAFTS[kind].width * TILE_PX - 8` by `FLOOR_PX - 12`, doors as two panels, ceiling light strip, cast shadow 4 px above at 25 percent.
   b. Everything else (condo, fastFood, restaurant, shop, cinema, partyHall, medical, security, housekeeping, parkingRamp, parkingSpace, recycling, metro, cathedral): draw the interior through a 2x scale so the old 8 px art fills the new cell pixel for pixel doubled. Use a Graphics scale of 2 on the interior only, with the natively authored shell around it. These are re-authored kind by kind in ship L3. A mixed grid must not exist: after this ship there is no 8 px assumption anywhere.
2. `bake()` in art.ts: `resolution: Math.min(window.devicePixelRatio || 1, 2)` rounded to 1 or 2, `scaleMode: 'nearest'` stays. The renderer's own `resolution` and `autoDensity` must match (check `createRenderer` options). The cache key does not need the resolution because it is fixed for the life of the renderer.
3. src/render/camera.ts: `DEFAULT_ZOOM` 2 to 1 (a floor is 72 CSS px), `SNAP_ZOOMS` stays `[0.5, 1, 2, 3]`, `MIN_ZOOM` 0.35 to 0.175 (the same 12.6 CSS px per floor at full zoom out), `MAX_ZOOM` 3. The initial camera y and the ground line rule stay in floors and fractions, so they need no change; check `reset()`.
4. Grep src/render, src/game, src/ui, src/site and tests for literal 8, 16, 24, 36, 32 used as tile or floor pixels, and for `/ 8`, `* 8`, `/ 36`, `* 36`. Replace with the constants. Pointer hit testing in src/game (px to tile and floor) and the placement chip maths in src/ui/ui.ts are the likely places. List what you changed.
5. The landing hero (src/site/hero.ts, smoke world from src/render/smoke.ts) draws with the same renderer: check it still frames the demo tower at 1440 and 390 wide and adjust its zoom or camera call if it now shows a quarter of the tower.
6. Tests: fix expectations that encoded 8 or 36 by deriving from the constants. tests/render/sim-art.test.ts, floors.test.ts, camera.test.ts, tests/ui/placement.test.ts and layout.test.ts are the likely ones. Add one test that `TILE_PX === 16 && FLOOR_PX === 72` and that no drawer's baked texture size differs from `tiles * TILE_PX` by `floors * FLOOR_PX` (plus the slab's 4 px shadow).
7. docs/VISUAL.md Tokens: update the slab line (2 px edge, cast shadow), the outline (2 px), the sims line (16 by 48 px), the principle 5 note on zoom, and add a line that the base grid is 16 px tiles and 72 px floors since 0.4.0.
8. Screenshots as above: game at default zoom, game at 0.5, landing at 1440 and 390.

Report: files and line ranges, the literal replacements from step 4, the hero adjustment, test counts, hashes, the screenshot paths, and any fork.

## Ship L2: light and time, and the low horizon (0.4.1)

1. Light layer: one screen sized `Sprite(Texture.WHITE)` in a new `layers.light` container added to `app.stage` after `worldRoot` and before the overlay layer (selection, ghost), `blendMode = 'multiply'`, alpha 0.4, tinted by minute of day: 06:00 `#bfd8ff`, 07:00 to 17:00 `#ffffff`, 18:00 `#ffd0a0`, 19:00 to 05:00 `#6078b0`, linear between anchors, updated once per game minute (Astra section 2). It must not tint the DOM chrome (it is inside the canvas, so it cannot). Resize with the viewport.
2. Window state: extend the room texture key's `lit` boolean to a state `day | lit | vacant | housekeeping`. day: panes `#7fb6e0`. lit (night, occupied): `#ffd866` with a 2 px `#fff3b0` header. vacant (night, no tenant): `#2a3550` entirely. housekeeping (night, dirty hotel room): lamp light only. Lobby lighting follows people present on that tile's floor. The renderer chooses the state per room from the world (tenant present, hotel dirty flag) and the hour; the reconcile from the engine round runs a full pass when the lit state flips (it already has `lastLitState`, extend it to the hour band). Bake the lit variants for every kind present in the tower at boot (a loop over `world.rooms` kinds and sizes calling `art.room` for each state) so the first dusk does not bake mid play.
3. Cars: lit interior (the ceiling light strip and a warm fill `#fff3b0` at 30 percent inside the cavity), a 2 px cable `#3b3f47` from the car's top to the shaft top, drawn per car as a Graphics in `layers.cars` under the car sprite and resized as the car moves, and the soft cast shadow up the shaft from L1.
4. Backdrop, the low horizon (decision 2026-09-22, no tall city): replace the single 24 px skyline strip in src/render/sky.ts with two parallax bands: far hills `#c7d6e3` (a smooth low curve, 40 px high at zoom 1, parallax factor 0.15 of camera x) and near low buildings `#b9cfe0` (two to four storey silhouettes, flat roofs, 64 px high, factor 0.3), both tinted toward night by `nightness`. Clouds: six soft white ellipses at 55 percent alpha drifting right to left at 4 CSS px per second, wrapping, factor 0.1, drawn in `layers.cityFar`; under `prefers-reduced-motion` or the in-game reduced motion switch (ui.ts `readReducedMotion`) clouds are static. Bands sit on the ground line and scroll vertically with the camera at their factor so they stay near the horizon.
5. Tests: sky colour at each anchor minute, light tint interpolation at 06:30 and 18:30, the window state chooser for the four states from a stub world, and a bake-at-boot test that after createRenderer with a world of two kinds the cache holds day and lit entries for both.
6. Screenshots: game at 12:00, 18:30 and 23:00 (set the world minute over CDP through the game api if exposed, else load saves at those minutes made with the scratchpad `tower.ts` builder), landing at 1440.

Report as L1.

## Ship L3: motion and the remaining rooms (0.4.2)

1. Elevator doors slide: replace the two-state door texture (`doorsOpen` true or false, `DOOR_HOLD_MS`) with a door position 0 to 1 tweened over 240 ms each way (120 ms under reduced motion is not enough of a saving; under reduced motion doors snap as today). Draw doors as two panel sprites clipped by a mask over the cavity, or bake five door frames (0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1) and pick the nearest; choose the frames route (no masks, no per-frame Graphics). Cars ease into a stop: the Motion class from the engine round interpolates linearly per tick; add an ease only on the last tick before a stop (the sim knows when a car arrives; if the renderer cannot tell, skip this and say so).
2. People: a three frame walk cycle (stand, stride, mirrored stride at 120 ms per frame, Astra section 1), an idle sway when waiting (1 px vertical bob every 800 ms), an impatience shuffle at the red stress band (2 px horizontal jitter every 400 ms), and outfit variety chosen from the sim id (hat, bag, coat, four colour sets) so it is stable across frames and saves. The particle path (the crowd atlas) must carry the same frames; if the atlas cannot carry the outfit variants within its size budget, sprites get variants and particles get the three frames only, and say so.
3. Build feedback: the 4 px settle from VISUAL.md stays; add a dust puff (six 2 px particles fading over 300 ms at the room's base) and a 200 ms soft white flash on the new room (a white sprite over the room, alpha 0.5 to 0). Both off under reduced motion.
4. Ambient life, all off under reduced motion: shop sign that blinks at night (a 2 px amber strip alternating every 900 ms), restaurant steam (three 2 px particles rising 12 px over 1.2 s), cinema marquee (a 4 px strip cycling three colours every 600 ms). Implemented as a small `ambient.ts` module in src/render that owns a list of emitters attached to rooms of those kinds and ticks on the frame with elapsed real time.
5. Rooms re-authored natively at 16 px (removing the 2x scale from L1 class b): condo, fastFood, restaurant, shop, cinema, partyHall, medical, security, housekeeping, parkingRamp, parkingSpace, recycling, metro, cathedral. Keep each kind's wall colour from VISUAL.md, add the two-tone shadow face, and give each interior twice the detail it had (a condo gets a sofa, a window with curtains and a kitchenette; a shop gets shelves and a counter; the cinema gets rows of seats and a screen; metro gets a platform edge and a train silhouette; cathedral gets pews, an aisle and a rose window). After this ship the 2x scale helper is deleted.
6. Tests: door frame picker by position, walk frame by elapsed time, outfit variant is a pure function of id, ambient emitters are absent under reduced motion, and the existing sim-art test extended to the three frames.
7. Screenshots: game at default zoom with a mixed tower (the scratchpad save), and a 2x zoom crop on a hotel and an office floor.

Report as L1.

## Ship L5: sound (0.4.4)

1. src/audio/audio.ts: a Web Audio synthesiser with no audio files. Effects: elevator chime (two sine notes, 660 and 880 Hz, 80 ms each), door (a 60 ms filtered noise burst), build thunk (a 40 Hz sine with a fast decay plus a short noise click), cash register on rent day (three quick clicks and a bell at 1320 Hz), soft alert for events (a 220 Hz triangle, 150 ms, twice), star sting (an ascending arpeggio C E G, 90 ms each). Ambient bed by hour: traffic (brown noise low passed at 300 Hz) from 07:00 to 19:00 at minus 30 dB, crickets (a 4 kHz sine pulsed at 12 Hz, gated randomly) from 21:00 to 05:00 at minus 34 dB, crossfaded over one game hour.
2. Wiring: the game shell exposes an event stream the UI already reads for toasts and the log (find it in src/game/api.ts); the audio module subscribes to it and to the clock for the ambient bed. The AudioContext is created on the first pointer or key event (browsers block it before a gesture) and stays suspended until the master toggle is on.
3. Settings: master toggle (off by default, stored under `hs.sound` next to the reduced motion key), effects and ambient sliders 0 to 100 stored under `hs.sound.effects` and `hs.sound.ambient`. When the master toggle is off nothing is created.
4. Tests: the synth schedules the right number of oscillator nodes per effect against a stub AudioContext, the ambient chooser by hour, the settings keys round trip, and that with the toggle off no AudioContext is constructed.
5. Screenshot: the settings panel with the sound section.

Report as L1.
