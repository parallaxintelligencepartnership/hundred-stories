# UX round spec

Date: 2026-09-22. Implements docs/reviews/2026-09-22-modernisation-proposal.md section 5 in three ships, each a senior-implementer delegation. Builds on the look round (status bar, palette tiles, panel header pattern from ship L4; the light layer and overlays share the canvas). Every ship: `npm test`, `npm run typecheck`, six hashes unchanged, `npm run build`, screenshots per the look round spec's method. Nothing under src/sim changes unless a ship says so. Copy follows docs/VISUAL.md principle 3 (the interface's voice, sentence case, US spelling, no em dashes) and stays short: a tip is one sentence plus an action.

Storage keys: everything in this round that remembers a choice uses localStorage under `hs.` next to the reduced motion, sound and theme keys, and is read through one small module src/ui/prefs.ts (get, set, with try/catch so a blocked storage never throws). Existing keys (`hundredStories.reducedMotion`, `hs.palette.collapsed`, `hs.hintSeen`) are read through the same module without renaming them.

## Ship U1: first run and goals (0.5.0)

1. Intro card on the first new game: three screens in the existing panel or bottom sheet pattern (src/ui/panels.ts): what the game is (a tower, tenants, rent, stars), what a star is (population and one landmark per step, from src/sim/stars.ts STARS), what kills a tower (stress from waiting, noise from bad neighbours, an elevator that reaches nothing). Next and Skip on every screen, one tap dismisses, `hs.intro.seen` set on dismiss or finish, never shown again; re-openable from settings under Help.
2. Guided first tower: a side card (desktop: the query panel slot; phone: the bottom sheet, one at a time with the palette) with five steps that advance on the real event, not a timer, read from the world after each notify: (1) build a lobby (a lobby room exists), (2) add an office (an office exists), (3) add an elevator that reaches it (a shaft whose floor range covers the office's floor and the lobby floor), (4) wait for the first tenants (any room occupied), (5) reach the population for the second star (`world.stars >= 2`). Each step lights the target palette row (a `hs-tool-hint` class on the tile) and highlights the valid placement band in the world through a renderer call `renderer.setGuideBand(floorMin, floorMax, xMin, xMax) | null` drawn in the overlay layer as a translucent amber band. Skippable at any step; finishing or skipping sets `hs.guide.done`. The card is offered only when the intro was shown this session or the tower is empty.
3. Contextual tips, each once (`hs.tips` holds a list of fired ids): a hall call waited over five minutes, a tenant left (with the leave reason from src/sim/evaluation.ts leaveReasonFor), the first quarter's rent landed, the first event fired, night speed kicked in (the auto 8x; the tip names the mode shown in the status bar), a panel opened for the first time. Each tip is one sentence in the toast slot with a "Got it" button that records the id. Tips do not fire while the intro or a guide step card is open; they queue and show one at a time.
4. Goals card: once the guide is done or skipped, the same card slot shows the next star's requirements as a checklist with live progress (population n of m, landmark built or not), plus one nudge when a requirement stalls: population unchanged for two game days while rooms are vacant ("Tenants need an elevator within reach"), or more than a dozen people waiting over five minutes in the last hour ("12 people waited over 5 minutes for a car this hour"). The counts come from the world; if the world does not keep the waited-over-five-minutes count per hour, add a small ring counter to the world that is not serialized and not hashed (like structureVersion) and is bumped in the people pass where the wait is measured. Say in the report which fields you added.
5. Help entry: the settings panel gets a Help section with Intro (reopens the card), Controls (the list from ship U3, or the current hint text until then) and a link to /how-to-play/.
6. Tests: intro shows once and never again from a stub storage; guide steps advance on the world events from a stub world and not on time; each tip fires once; goals checklist from a stub world at stars 1 and 3; the nudge conditions; prefs module never throws with a storage that throws.
7. Screenshots: intro screen 1 at 1440 and 390, guide step 3 with the band visible, goals card at stars 2.

## Ship U2: information layers (0.5.1)

1. Overlay toggles on the status bar: stress heat, noise, vacancy, elevator wait. One active at a time or none; a segmented control with the label "View" and four sentence-case options plus Off. Rendering: a per-room tint layer in the renderer (a Graphics per visible room, or a tint pass over the room sprites when the overlay is active; the overlay is exempt from the structure-version gate and redraws every frame while active) with a five step colour ramp per view: stress and noise from the room's evaluation and noisy neighbours (src/sim/evaluation.ts), vacancy as occupied or vacant, elevator wait per floor from the hall call ages. A legend appears in the status bar while a view is on. Off by default and not remembered.
2. Hover previews: hovering a shaft (desktop) or tapping it (phone, the same tap that selects) shows a small inline card by the pointer with waiting counts per floor served and the car settings; hovering a room shows evaluation, rent and tenant count. The query panel keeps the full detail; the hover card is the preview and is positioned by the placement chip's cached measurement logic from the engine round, never a forced layout per frame.
3. Placement ghost: the ghost already shows footprint; add for a shaft the floors it will serve (a band on the ghost) and for a room a one line explainer under the refusal chip for the common refusals: not enough cash (names the cost), no floor below (needs the floor under it built), overlaps, out of the tower, needs a star (names it). Map the refusal reasons from src/sim/build.ts CommandResult to those lines; unknown reasons keep the raw text.
4. Tests: the ramp function for each view from a stub world, one active overlay at a time, the legend appears and clears, hover card content for a shaft and a room, the refusal explainer mapping.
5. Screenshots: stress view at 1440, elevator wait view, a hover card on a shaft, the refusal explainer for not enough cash.

## Ship U3: controls and the minimap (0.5.2)

1. Keyboard: number keys 1 to 9 select palette groups in the palette's order (the first tool of the group goes in hand), letters select tools within the active group (the tile shows its letter), Escape clears the tool, Space pauses and resumes. Keys are shown in the tile tooltips (`title`) and in the Help controls list. Existing keys (arrows pan, plus and minus zoom) stay; check src/ui/ui.ts for the current key handling and src/render/input.ts, and make sure typing in any text field does not trigger them.
2. Minimap: when the tower is taller than the viewport (tower height in floors times floor px at the current zoom exceeds the free band), a 96 px wide minimap in the bottom right of the view (above the ticker) shows the tower as one pixel row per floor (a width proportional to the built extent, a colour per room kind at low saturation), the viewport as an outline, click or drag to move the camera. Hidden when the whole tower fits. Drawn on a small canvas from the world on structure change and the camera on every frame the camera moved (cheap: a few hundred rects). Phone: 64 px wide, the same rule.
3. Tests: key to tool mapping, keys ignored in inputs, minimap visibility rule at three tower heights, minimap click maps to camera y.
4. Screenshots: the minimap on a 30 floor tower at 1440 (build one with the scratchpad tower builder), the tile tooltip.
