## Suspicions

### S1. A repeated demolish refusal whose notice has no follow-up sentence is swallowed after the first tap (proposed: IMPORTANT)
- Where: src/ui/ui.ts:1486 (the `line.text === lastNoticeText` skip runs before the `demolishNotice` branch at 1489). src/ui/explain.ts:142-148 maps fire, bomb, cars, condo and "nothing" to an empty follow-up, so `notice()` stores the bare reason as `lastNoticeText` (ui.ts:1611).
- Input: pick Demolish, then tap a burning room (`onFire = true`) three times. The same happens for "Deal with the bomb first.", "Wait until the cars are empty." and the sold-condo reason.
- Wrong outcome: tap 1 shows a notice card. On tap 2 the log line equals `lastNoticeText`, so it is skipped as "already said". `newestNoticed` stays false, and the line falls into the warning-toast branch, which shows a news toast. Tap 3 falls into the same branch, but the 20 s GIVE_UP_TOAST_GAP_MS throttle drops it, so the player sees nothing at all. The commit's stated intent is a notice "every time", which "the warning toast's gap... cannot swallow". The shipped test only covers "People are inside.", whose notice text differs from the log line, so it never reaches this path.
- Reproduce: scratch probe `c38/tests/zprobe/refusal-repeat.test.ts` (the demolish-refusal harness, with the room armed two ways). Output:
  `fire | tap1: notices=1 toasts=0 | tap2: notices=1 toasts=1 | tap3: notices=1 toasts=1`
  `people (control) | tap1: notices=1 | tap2: notices=2 | tap3: notices=3 toasts=0`
