## Verified (attacks that did not land)
- CSP: script-src 'self' covers /splash-init.js and nothing is inline. Across the three engines with the _headers CSP applied, the console showed zero CSP violations. Media and poster fall under default-src 'self'.
- Cache header: /splash-init.js has `no-cache, must-revalidate`, the same as theme.js.
- Service worker: the scratch build precached 27 entries, including neither splash-init.js, any trailers/ file nor the landing index.html. It keeps play/index.html. vite.config.ts:139-167.
- dist-app has only assets, fonts, icons, index.html and theme.js. `grep -rl splash dist-app` is empty. The built play page loads theme.js, play-*.js, the beacon and registerSW, so /play/ is untouched.
- Range: run_worker_first "/trailers/*" and range.ts serve any file under the prefix. The sitemap and robots need no entry (other trailers are not listed either). make-design-sheet and make-hero-still set hs.splash before the landing loads. The other `${base}/` navigations are the game at root (dist-app).
- Gate: under reduced motion, no splash-pending and zero /trailers/ requests in all engines. sessionStorage throwing (getter throws SecurityError) gave no class, no overlay, and the hero still drew. The same-session reload showed no splash.
- Play path: the poster and mp4 were requested only on the play path. The splash ended naturally after 19.5 s (chromium), 19.7 s (webkit) and 20.9 s (firefox), leaving the class dropped, overlay removed and scroll working. Escape ended it with teardown, and focus moved on to the wordmark.
- No-module failsafe (main-*.js aborted): at 2 s the overlay was visible with overflow hidden. At 31 s it was `visibility:hidden`, overflow visible, the wheel scrolled 600 and hit-testing reached the page. Overflow animates discretely in chromium, firefox and webkit (failsafe.cjs).
- hero.ts boots under the overlay: chromium reported data-hero-settled=drawn with has-canvas while the video played at 1.5 s.
- theme.js stays the first head script, and splash-init is a second sync classic script after it (clips.test.ts:136). US English in the visitor copy ("neighbors"). British spellings appear in comments only, and DECISIONS 2026-09-24 leaves comments alone.
- The trailer copy claims hold: site-intro.mp4 runs 30.000 s with no audio stream (ffprobe). Red stress marks exist (figure.ts MARK_COLOURS.bang). Stairs add stress per floor (people.ts:554).
- The splash tests prove behavior. Each end path, the poster-only-on-play path, the throwing storage, the gate branches and the single teardown all fail on the obvious mutation (the assertions check the fake DOM state directly).
