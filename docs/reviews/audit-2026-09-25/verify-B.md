## Verdicts

### S1. Offices lease, pay rent and count toward population when their workers can never reach them - CONFIRMED (final: CRITICAL)
- Reproduction: verifyB/s1.ts builds a lobby, offices on floors 2 and 3, and one standard shaft from 1 to 3, then runs to 05:01 on day 3 (the next quarter start). It runs twice: once with the car set to `hotel` and once left `any`.
  - hotel: `[480] An office on floor 2/3 was rented`. At 12:00 each day: `population 12`, `vacant false/false`, `occ 0/0`, `eval 1.00/1.00`, `workers {"outside":12}`. `maxOfficeOccupancySeen 0`. Quarter: `lastQuarter {"income":20000,"upkeep":10000,"net":10000}`, cash +10,000.
  - any (control): the same lease minute, `occ 6/6`, `workers {"inRoom":12}`, and the same `income 20000`, cash +10,000, population 12.
  - Cash, population and stars come out identical whether or not a single worker can get in. The cause is `people.ts:128`: `isReachableFromLobby` goes to `routing.ts:565` `findRoute` with no riderClass, so it uses the class-blind graph. Meanwhile `startTrip` (people.ts:295) uses `riderClass:'office'`, gets null, and the worker stays outside. Outside stress decays (people.ts:587), so eval stays at 1.0 and `moveOut` never fires. The lease, and the rent, go on forever. Stored cash and `world.population` (and so stars) are wrong for a real player setup. That meets the CRITICAL rubric.
- Fix spec: `isReachableFromLobby` takes a rider class (cache keyed by floor and class). `fillVacantOffices` passes `'office'`, `spawnHotelGuests` passes `'hotel'`, and commerce and show intake pass `'other'`. Also, a tenant whose trip to its own home room fails for want of a route must build stress or set a leave reason, so that `tickEvaluation` vacates an office that became unreachable after it was leased (a car setting changed later). Test: a scenario test (people.test.ts mocks routing, so it can't host this) with the only car set to `hotel`. After one weekday morning, expect the office `vacant === true` and `populationOf === 0`. This fails today. A second case leases, then flips the car, and expects vacancy within `EVAL.leaveAfterMinutes` plus a day. Must not change: towers whose cars are all `any` (the class graph equals the blind graph there), so the bench hashes hold.

### S2. Turning off a stop strands the riders already aboard for good, and the shaft can then never be demolished - CONFIRMED (final: IMPORTANT)
- Reproduction: verifyB/s2.ts sets up a natural tower (offices on 2 and 3, a fast food on 4, a shaft from 1 to 5). A diner 78 boards at 11:41 for floor 4, and then I turn stop 4 off. Readings at +1, +10, +60, +600, +1440, +2880 and +4320 minutes are all the same: `state riding inCar 65 stress 0.02 route {ride 1->4} car y 1 idle passengers [78] calls [4]`. `shaft.demolish` returns `Wait until the cars are empty.` and `shaft.removeCar` returns `An elevator needs at least one car.` Turning stop 4 back on frees the diner 4 minutes later.
- Rules checked, none of which frees the rider: `updateStress` only touches waiting and in-room sims. `runSchedules` skips riding sims (line 255). `runLeaving` only runs for `leaving`. `carFloors` (elevators.ts:216) and `shouldStop` (line 261) drop floor 4. `serveFloor` alights only at `leg.toFloor`. So the only way out is to undo the stop change, and the demolish refusal never says so.
- Fix spec: `doSetStop(stops:false)` refuses while any car of the shaft carries a passenger whose `route[0]` rides to that floor, with a plain reason ("People are riding to floor N. Try again when they are off."), the same way `setCarRange` and `removeCar` guard. Test: in cars.test.ts, a rider aboard for 4, then `setStop 4 off` expects `ok:false`. That fails today. Must not change: setStop on a shaft with no riders bound for that floor.

### S3. A sim on its way out waits forever once its floor loses its only route - CONFIRMED (final: IMPORTANT)
- Reproduction: verifyB/s3.ts sets up a natural tower (an office on 2, a fast food on 3, a shaft from 1 to 3). At d0 12:52, diner 71 is really waiting on 3 with `exiting true` and a ride leg from 3 to 1. Then I turn stop 3 off. Readings at +18/+19 (the first reroute): still waiting. At +60: stress 1.00. At +600, +1440, +2880 and +4320: `state waiting stress 1.00 leaveReason null exiting true`, with the stale hall call `[3]` left in place. The give-up is blocked by `!sim.exiting` (people.ts:582). `rerouteWaitingSim` returns early on a null route (line 617). The midnight reset only clears `leaveReason`, and that doesn't help a sim with `exiting` set. Nothing ever ends it.
- Fix spec: in `rerouteWaitingSim`, when `findRoute` is null and the sim is exiting (or is transient), call `finishLeave`. That matches runLeaving:697. Test: in long-waits.test.ts or a scenario test, set up this case and expect the sim gone within `HALL_CALL_RETRY_MINUTES*RETRIES_BEFORE_REROUTE + 1` minutes. That fails today. Must not change: an exiting sim with a valid route still waits for its car and never repeats a give-up.

### S4. A tenant removed while sitting in another room leaves a phantom person in that room - CONFIRMED (final: raised to CRITICAL)
- Reproduction, demolish path (verifyB/s4.ts `demolish condo`, natural play at 3 stars; the log labels in that file still say worker/fast food/office, but the rooms are condo/restaurant): at d1 18:36 a resident is in the restaurant and its condo's occupancy is 0. Demolish succeeds, and then: `ff occupancy 1 real 0`. At 03:00 on d2, d3 and d4 it is still `occupancy 1 real 0`. Demolishing the restaurant with nobody in it returns `People are inside.`
- Fire path (s4.ts `fire`): two workers are at lunch when their office burns (helicopter), giving `ff occupancy 2 real 0` every night after that.
- Population path (verifyB/s4h.ts): a housekeeper is cleaning hotel room 108 when the housekeeping office burns. After that, hotel room 108 reads `occ 1 real 0 tenants 0` on d1, d2 and d3. `populationOf 1` against 0 rooms that really have guests, and `world.population 1`. Demolishing that room returns `People are inside.`
- `occupancy` is saved (save.ts:563), and nothing recounts it. Why I raised it: it inflates stored population (hotel rooms with `occupancy>0` count toward population and stars in stars.ts:18-22). It also makes a room permanently impossible to demolish with a false reason, and it takes commerce seats away.
- Fix spec: in `evictInto` (events.ts:134-137) and in `doDemolish`'s tenant loop (build.ts:456-468), when `sim.inRoomId` points at a different room, decrement that room's occupancy the way `departRoom` does. Also add a load-time (or hourly) recount of occupancy from the sims so towers that already carry phantoms heal. Test: after demolish, fire and bomb, assert that for every room `occupancy === count(sims with inRoomId === room.id, excluding guard/collector)`. That fails today. Must not change: guards and collectors stay uncounted.
