## Verdicts

### S1. Demolishing a burning room leaves a fire that never ends and blocks arrivals - CONFIRMED (final: IMPORTANT, lowered from CRITICAL)
- Reproduction (v1.ts): a real tower (200 lobby tiles, 126 offices on floors 2 to 7, 2 fast foods, condos, 2 elevators with 3 cars each). I ran one day so stars rose to 2 on their own (population 807). At 06:01 on day 1 I called the real `startFire` (seed 2). It picked office id 331 on floor 7, occupancy 0. `demolish` returned `{ok:true}`. Afterwards `events = [{"kind":"fire","roomIds":[331],...}]`, room 331 is gone, and 0 rooms are on fire. For the next **10 game days** at 06:01 the event was still `["fire"]`, with `newIds=0` (no sim of any kind was created), 801 people outside, 0 diners, and no condo sales. The only cash change was the quarterly office rent (+1,247,000 twice). The no-fire control from the same snapshot made 295 to 614 new sims a day and about $6,000 a day in food income. The phantom fire also survives serialize and deserialize.
- Escapes (the reviewer's severity question): (A) at 2 stars, building a $100,000 security office was accepted and the fire ended in 1 minute. The log said "Security put the fire out. 0 rooms burned down and clearing the damage cost $0." (B) `fire.callHelicopter` was accepted: cash went down by 250,000 and the event cleared. The fire alert tells the player "Call a helicopter or wait for security", so the escape is named. Fires only start at 2 stars or more, and the security office unlocks at 2 stars. At 3 stars and up a security office exists (3 stars requires one), so the phantom fire ends at once.
- Why not CRITICAL: the save is intact, nothing diverges, and population and stars held in this run. What the player loses is income while the fire lasts. It is a stuck event with a named, affordable escape. That makes it "an event with no path to ending" by itself, which is IMPORTANT. A narrow worse case (not reproduced): a hotel-heavy 2-star tower loses hotel population while guests are held outside, which could drop it to 1 star. The security office would then be refused, and the only way out is $250,000 for the helicopter.
- Fix spec: in `tickFire` (events.ts:218), end the fire when `event.roomIds` has no room left in `world.rooms` (or, alternatively, refuse `demolish` on a room with `onFire`; which one is the owner's question). Test: start a fire the way startFire does, demolish the room, tick 1 minute, and expect `world.events` to have no fire and arrivals to resume. It fails today. Must not change: fire spread, security and helicopter endings on live fires, the 2026-09-24 "no arrivals while a fire burns" rule, or the hashes of fire-free replays.

### S3. Multi-floor basements refused when their top floor sits under a built floor - CONFIRMED (final: IMPORTANT)
- Reproduction (v3.ts): lobby on floor 1 at x100 to 159, parking ramps on B1 at x100, 116 and 132, stars 5. `canBuild(recycling, -3, 100)` (covers B3 and B2) and `canBuild(metro, -4, 100)` (covers B4 to B2) both return "Build a floor below this one first." I read build.ts myself. `hasSupport` (196-201) gets the base floor. For floor < -1 it tests `floorIsBuilt(floor + 1)`: that is B2 for recycling at -3, which is the room's own top floor, and B3 for the metro at -4. `restsOnStructure` (227-259) would pass, since `restingFloor` gives top + 1 = -1 and the ramps overlap. game.ts:518/614 passes the pointer floor as the base, so the player hits this by pointing at B3. Workarounds: a parking space on B2 makes recycling at -3 pass, but the metro at -4 is still refused. A shaft from -3 to 1 makes both pass. The message points to neither. Recycling is required for 4 stars and the metro for 5.
- Fix spec: `hasSupport` should test the floor over the span's top for basements (`spanTop(floor, height) + 1`, with -1 meaning the lobby), or be dropped for multi-floor rooms in favor of `restsOnStructure`. Test: the v3 layout, recycling at -3 and metro at -4 x100, expect ok. Both fail today. Must not change: parking at -3 with B2 empty is still refused, and the tests at build.test.ts:281-291 and 294-299 keep passing.

### S5. A shaft can float anywhere and hold up rooms - PARTIAL (final: ADVISORY pending the owner)
- What holds (v3.ts, empty lot, no lobby): `shaft.build standard x200 floors 40..69` returned ok. `office 41 x198` returned ok (`isHeldUp=true` on the shaft at floor 40), and `office 42 x190` returned ok. `office 41 x300`, off the shaft, was refused with "Nothing is holding this up." The shaft then cannot be demolished ("Something above rests on this."). p4 also reproduced the underground case, a shaft from B10 to B2 with a parking space at -3.
- What does not hold as a clear defect: the 2026-09-24 line says "a room needs at least one tile of structure directly under it" and never defines "structure". Its plain reading neither includes nor excludes a shaft. The code comment at build.ts:222-225 counts a shaft passing the floor as structure. The 2026-09-19 decision lets shafts "rise into floors that do not exist yet" and declined "shafts needing existing floors at both ends". A rule requiring one supported end is a different rule, but it sits close to the declined option. DESIGN section 2 says a shaft may rise "from the lobby into empty air".
- Question for the owner: "Does an elevator shaft count as structure that a room can stand on? And must the shaft itself start from something built (the lobby, a room, or a basement over it), or may a shaft stand alone in the sky or underground and carry rooms?"
- Fix spec (only if the owner says a shaft needs its own support): `canBuildShaft` requires its bottom floor (its top floor for an all-basement shaft) to rest on structure or the lobby. Test: the v3 S5 commands expect the shaft to be refused. Existing towers are grandfathered.
