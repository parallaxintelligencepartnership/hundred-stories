Clips

Four short films from inside the tower. One is the trailer. The other three are the moments players talk about most, and every one of them will happen in your tower sooner or later.

B1

The trailer

Thirty seconds, silent on purpose. An empty lot becomes a tower, the tower fills with people, and every floor gets a story of its own. This is the game the way it plays in your browser.

B2

Short clips

Three things that happen in every tower. They are a lot funnier when they happen to somebody else.

The elevator is out

One person on floor 1 looks at the line for the elevator, looks at the stairs, and makes a decision. A stumble on the second flight. A burst through the door on floor 4, out of breath, just in time to watch the elevator doors open and everyone else stroll out.

In the game, people take the stairs when the wait for a car is too long, and it costs them. Every flight climbed adds stress, and stressed tenants leave. One more car in the shaft is usually the cure.

The backlog

A tenant sets one bag outside the door. Then another. A week goes by and the pile is taller than the neighbors, who lean away from it as they hurry past. It sways, it tips, and a collection worker with a cart makes the catch of the season, then sweeps up and takes a bow.

In the game, the rooms people use make waste every morning. The recycling center's collection workers ride the elevators to pick it up. Let it pile up two mornings in a row and the room counts as dirty, and dirty rooms drag down their rating.

The wait

A full lobby, one car, and every eye on the floor indicator. Then the ding. The doors open, and one person strolls out in no hurry at all. A double take, a whole crowd's shoulders drop, and the doors close again.

In the game, every minute spent waiting for a car adds stress, and you can see it over each person's head. When the lobby crowd wears red marks, add a car, add a shaft, or run an express car to the busiest floors.
